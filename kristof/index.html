Getting here

Arriving and getting around

By air

The short flight from San José to Cóbano takes about 23 minutes. We can help book your Green Airways flight and meet you at the airstrip.

By road

Prefer to drive? We arrange private air-conditioned transfers. Ask us for the fare.

Around town

Most guests rent an ATV, the easiest way around Santa Teresa. UTV buggies and 4x4s are also available.

Plan to arrive with time to settle in before dinner. Bring some cash: USD is accepted, and colones are handy for tips and small purchases.

How to use it

Light. Heat. Cold. Rest.

About 20 min

The gentle reset

Red light mask 8–12 min
Cedar sauna 8–12 min
Cold plunge 30 s–1 min
Rest 2–3 min

30–45 min

The full circuit

Red light mask 8–12 min
Cedar sauna 15–20 min
Cold plunge 1–2 min
Repeat sauna and plunge 2–3 times

Experienced guests

The deep one

Cedar sauna 15–20 min
Cold plunge up to 3 min
Rest, then repeat 3 times

Always finish on cold; the reset happens as your body warms back up. Dry off before using the PEMF mat. Not in the mood for cold? Keep the plunge to 30 seconds or take a cool shower. Pacemaker, pregnancy, recent surgery or fever? Please check with us before using the PEMF mat, sauna or plunge.
